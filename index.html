Porchlight Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

The short version

  • Porchlight has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your profile and status live in your own iCloud.
  • Your status is shared only with people you invite, and you can stop sharing with anyone at any time.
  • We, the developer, cannot see your status, your photo, your friends or your contacts.
  • We don't sell data, don't use analytics, and don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • The free version shows non-personalized ads from Google AdMob. Remove Ads or Porchlight Pro turns ads off completely.

Who we are

Porchlight is an iPhone app made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. For questions, see Contact.

Information you share with your people

To show your people when you're free, Porchlight uses the information you enter in the app:

  • your name and, if you add one, your photo;
  • your status (Free to FaceTime, Free to Call, Unavailable or off) and when it ends;
  • your time zone, if you choose Automatic or Manual (you can hide it);
  • the FaceTime address and phone number you enter, so people can call you;
  • your chosen card style.

This information is stored in your private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit. For each person you invite, Porchlight creates a separate private iCloud share that only that person can open. If you show your status only to certain people, or hide it from someone, the people who shouldn't see it receive "Unknown" instead: your real status never reaches their device.

We have no access to your iCloud data. It is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Information about your people

When someone shares their Porchlight status with you, their name, photo, status, time zone (if they share it) and contact details are downloaded from iCloud to your device. You can remove anyone at any time; removing a Porchlight friend also stops them from seeing you.

Contacts

Porchlight never asks for access to your contacts. When you add someone from Contacts, or link a friend to a contact, you pick one contact with Apple's contact picker and only that contact's name, photo, phone number and email address are given to the app. They are stored only on your device and are not uploaded or shared with anyone.

Information stored on your device

Porchlight keeps a copy of your profile, your people and your settings on your iPhone so the app and its widgets work quickly and offline. This includes favorites, notification choices and your appearance setting. It stays on your device.

Notifications

If you turn on "Notify me when they're free", Porchlight shows a notification on your device when that person's status changes. iCloud tells the app that something changed; the notification itself is created on your device.

Focus

If you add Porchlight as a Focus filter (for example for Do Not Disturb), iOS tells Porchlight when that Focus turns on or off so it can change your status. Porchlight does not read your Focus settings otherwise.

Purchases

Remove Ads and Porchlight Pro are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details, name or Apple Account. The app only checks with Apple whether a purchase is active.

Advertising

The free version of Porchlight shows one ad, provided by Google AdMob. We request non-personalized ads only: ads are not based on a profile of your interests or on your activity in other apps, and Porchlight never asks for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency).

To show and measure ads, prevent fraud and limit how often you see the same ad, Google's software in the app may collect information such as your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), device and operating system information, app information, and how you interact with the ad. Google processes this information under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Porchlight asks for your consent using Google's consent form before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app.

If you buy Remove Ads or Porchlight Pro, the app stops requesting ads.

What we don't do

  • We don't have accounts, servers or databases that hold your information.
  • We don't use analytics or third-party tracking tools.
  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't use your information for personalized advertising.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics under Apple's policies.

Your choices and rights

  • Who sees you: choose who sees each status, hide your status from someone, or remove people, all in the app.
  • Stop sharing: Settings → Who can see you → Stop sharing with everyone removes access for everyone you invited.
  • Time zone: set it to Hidden in Settings or in Edit your card.
  • Ads: change your consent in Settings → Ad privacy choices (where available), or remove ads with a purchase.
  • Delete your data: stop sharing with everyone and delete the app. Data in your iCloud account can also be managed in your iPhone's iCloud settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal information. Because we don't hold your information ourselves, most of these rights are exercised directly in the app or with Apple. For information collected by Google for advertising, see Google's policies above. You can also contact us with any request.

Children

Porchlight is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Porchlight handles information, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy? Open an issue at github.com/cousinchronicles/porchlight-privacy.
